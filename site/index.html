Diabetes Risk Prediction

A reproducible machine-learning framework for early detection of type-2 diabetes risk using the Pima Indians Diabetes Database.

Abstract. Diabetes mellitus is one of the fastest-growing chronic diseases worldwide, and early identification of at-risk individuals is critical to clinical intervention. This project presents a complete, reproducible machine-learning framework for binary classification of diabetes status, with a strict stratified train / validation / test protocol, leakage-safe Pipeline-based preprocessing, class-imbalance-aware metrics (ROC-AUC, F1), and baseline benchmarking against a majority-class dummy classifier. A tuned Gradient Boosting model reaches a held-out ROC-AUC of ≈ 0.82 with 93% unit-test coverage and fully deterministic reproducibility from a single seed.
